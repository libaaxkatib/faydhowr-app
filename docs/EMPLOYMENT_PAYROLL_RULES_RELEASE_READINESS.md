# Employment and payroll rules - release readiness

> **Update 2026-10-26 (later the same day):** the owner answered the four questions in section 4. (1) Transfer stays the current two steps: Stop, then Assign. (2) Temporary payment: prefill the calculated amount, warn on a different amount, allow override, record both. (3) Temporary cost splits by the salary's effective date; the Replacement keeps the whole-month rule. (4) Temporary becomes Permanent only in the scenario where HR/Management chooses that Temporary to replace a departing Permanent, with the salary inherited automatically. The canonical rules, the traceability table and the remaining gaps are now in **`docs/HR_PAYROLL_RULES.md`**; this report stays as the dated audit snapshot.

> **Update 2026-10-26 (U8 and U9 final):** the owner then settled two points that change rows 4 and 5 below. **U8:** the Temporary coverage period (actual start to actual end, both inclusive) now determines both the Temporary's entitlement and the related deduction from the Permanent; verified attendance days no longer decide the amount (they stay an attendance record). The entitlement is calculated first; the deduction is the existing approved rule (the month's entitlement deducted once, after approval, never a day already unpaid leave). **U9:** `employment_type` changes to Permanent as soon as the selection is saved, while the new Permanent assignment, salary and payroll begin on the effective date HR selects, which is included and never shifted. Where rows 4 and 5 below say "verified worked days", read "the actual coverage period". Gaps 1 (payout versus entitlement), 2 (salary basis) and 4 (Temporary to Permanent) of section 3 are addressed by these decisions; a **Replacement** keeps its whole-month rule, and Temporaries still do not appear in the payment list. The current rules, code and tests are in **`docs/HR_PAYROLL_RULES.md`** (sections 3, 4 and 7).

> **Update 2026-10-27 (consolidated audit):** the remaining items were settled in one pass; see **`docs/HR_PAYROLL_RULES.md` section 6**. Resolved by the owner: Replace Employee keeps accepting any Active employee, no new payment-approval gate, gap days are allowed and not paid to the successor, and the Excel column `Verified workdays` keeps its name and meaning. Resolved by evidence: the Waiting List salary is always entered by hand (approved rule found), and rounding follows the approved once-per-month rule. One defect was fixed (a Temporary could be paid twice for the same day on two positions). Three owner decisions remain: Permanent to Temporary, the overlapping-cover policy, and whether the payment list should show Temporaries.

Scope: `main` at `2f4f96f` (live since 2026-10-26). Read-only audit; nothing was changed, committed, pushed, deployed or migrated. No production system was contacted.

## 3. Confirmed gaps (none is a defect in rules 1-3 or 5-7)

1. **Payout to a Temporary is not tied to the computed entitlement.** The cost is derived and shown, but `RecordTemporaryReplacementPaymentAction` accepts any typed amount with no comparison or cap, and the Temporary has no payroll line, so Temporaries do not appear in the monthly payment list. (Rule 4 as written is met; this is the next question the owner is likely to ask.)
2. **Two salary-basis rules for a mid-month change of the Permanent's salary.** A Replacement uses the new salary for the whole month (owner decision of 19 Oct, implemented). The Temporary cost calculator uses the salary in effect on each worked day (pinned by `test_the_salary_in_effect_on_each_worked_day_is_used`). The owner's 10 Oct wording ("applicable Permanent monthly salary") does not say which.
3. **No Transfer action.** Moving someone between facilities or companies is Stop plus Assign (two steps, both audited); a position change is an in-place edit. No owner statement defines the intended workflow, so this is neither wrong nor confirmed.
4. **Temporary to Permanent and back.** `employees.employment_type` (`permanent` or `temporary`) is editable with an audit entry showing from and to. A Temporary's own assignment gets no payroll line; a Replacement can be edited to Permanent, audited. No rule says what happens to an employee's existing assignments when `employment_type` flips, or how the days before and after are paid. The system warns on some combinations but there is no conversion workflow.
5. **Month-end timing and the Excel layout** are separate topics. Timing is not enforced (reminders deferred by the owner). The layout question (the owner's own `SALARY SEP 2026.xlsx` has separate absence, fine, customer and position-filling deduction columns plus a formula; the exports do not) is already in the recovery report and not repeated here.

None of these needs a database migration to answer. Gaps 1, 2 and 4 would be code changes after the owner decides; gap 3 would be a code change only if a Transfer action is wanted. No data correction is needed because no real data has been affected.

## 4. Owner questions (the minimum needed, each answerable in one line)

1. **Moving a Permanent employee:** is the current two-step flow (Stop the old assignment with a last day, then Assign the new one; position edited in place) what you want, or do you want one Transfer action that does both?
2. **Paying a Temporary:** should the amount be pre-filled from the verified cost and warn when it differs, or stay free-typed as now?
3. **Mid-month salary change of the Permanent:** for a Temporary's cost, use the new salary for the whole month (like a Replacement) or per day as now?
4. **Temporary becomes Permanent (or the reverse):** from which date does the new type apply, and does the person need a new assignment from that date?
