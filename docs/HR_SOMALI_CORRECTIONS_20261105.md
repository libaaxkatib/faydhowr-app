## Terminology decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Advance | **Lacag hormaris** everywhere (the locked label); the variants *lacag-hormarin* / *hormarin* are gone. |
| Correct (button/title) | **Saxo** (imperative, like the locked *Keydi, Hubi, Jooji, Ansixi*). *Sax* alone also means "right / OK" and could be read as a confirmation. Verb forms use *sax-* (*la saxay*), not *six-*. |
| Void | Action keeps the **locked** *Ka noqo*; the state keeps *La buriyey* (voided). The two words differ as they already do in the locked *Void payment* (*Ka noqo Lacag-bixinta*); the owner may prefer one root. |
| Balance | **haraag** (indefinite) / **haraaga** (definite) - fixes the *haraa* typo. |
| Owed / deducted / waived | *Weli lagu leeyahay* / *La jaray* / *La cafiyey* (unchanged). |
| Lines | **safaf** (as in the locked *Dhammaan safafka*) replaces *khadad*. |
| Legacy-locked section (#6) | Meaning preserved and made clearer: it was locked by an earlier version of the system and **must be finalized before it is paid**. |
