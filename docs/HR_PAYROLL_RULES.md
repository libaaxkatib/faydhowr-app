# HR & Payroll rules - Permanent, Temporary and Replacement

This is the **canonical rules document** for employment types, assignments, replacements and monthly salary calculation in the Fayadhowr HR & Marketing Web Panel. It replaces the scattered statements in owner messages, audit reports and code comments as the place to look. When a rule changes, change it here first, then the code and tests (the traceability table in section 7 lists both).

- Written 2026-10-26 against `main` = `2f4f96f` (production release) and updated since with the implementation on the local branch `feat/hr-permanent-temporary-rules` (not pushed, not deployed); the latest update (2026-10-27, "Consolidation and Closure") records the owner decisions on U1, U12 and the monthly payment list. A rule is implemented only where section 7 says **Implemented**.
- Location: `docs/`, topic-named like `FACILITY_DEEP_CLEANING_TRACKING.md`. `HRM_MARKETING_SRS.md` has no payroll or replacement rules (checked: no mention), so there was no earlier source of truth to update.
- Related audit snapshots (dated, not canonical): `docs/EMPLOYMENT_PAYROLL_RULES_RELEASE_READINESS.md` (2026-10-26).

**Evidence labels:** **[OWNER date]** = a written owner decision in the project's conversation records; **[CODE]** = behaviour of the implementation; **[TEST]** = pinned by an automated test; **[ASSUMED]** = implementation choice that no owner statement covers. A rule marked [CODE]/[TEST] only is recorded as behaviour, not as an owner decision.

## 1. Vocabulary (do not mix these)

| Term | Meaning in the system | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Permanent** | The regular holder of a position. An employee has `employment_type` `permanent` (default) or `temporary`, and each assignment has `assignment_type` `permanent` or `replacement`. | `employees.employment_type`, `employee_work_assignments.assignment_type` |
| **Temporary (employee)** | A person classified `temporary`. A Temporary has no pay line of their own; their pay is the **replacement cost**, worked out from verified worked days and deducted from the Permanent they covered. | `employees.employment_type = temporary` |
| **Temporary replacement** (cover) | A record that a Temporary covered a specific Permanent assignment, with its own start/end dates, a daily list of workdays (recorded, verified, voided), an approval, and payments. The Permanent assignment stays in place. | `temporary_replacements`, `temporary_replacement_workdays`, `ReplacementCostCalculator`, `ReplacementCostService` |
| **Replacement** (assignment type) | Someone who takes over the position: the Permanent's assignment ends and a new assignment of type `replacement` starts, linked by `replaces_assignment_id`. It has no salary of its own; it inherits the replaced Permanent's. | `AssignmentType::Replacement`, `CreateWorkAssignmentAction`, `ChangeAssignmentSalaryAction::applyToReplacements` |
| **Permanent successor** | The person chosen in Replace Employee with type Permanent: a normal Permanent assignment linked to the one it follows. | `ReplaceCompanyEmployeeAction` |

Temporary and Replacement are **not interchangeable** [OWNER 2026-10-19]. Where the owner's wording says "Temporary replacement" or "Replacement", this document maps it as above and flags any ambiguity in section 5.

## 3. Owner decisions of 2026-10-26

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| N1 | **Removing a Permanent employee from an assignment requires choosing the replacement first.** Allowed choices: (1) an existing Temporary employee who has been covering that Permanent position; (2) a person selected from the HR Waiting List. The removal must not complete while the choice is missing. No other choice is offered and nobody is selected automatically. It applies to every path that actually ends an open Permanent assignment: Stop, End, setting or shortening the end date, and employee Separation. It does **not** apply to Cancel (only possible for a mistaken assignment with no payroll and no covers, so no position was in service), to a Replacement-type assignment, or to Replace Employee (which names its own successor in the same action). A position whose successor assignment already exists is already resolved. |
| N2 | **Temporary becomes Permanent** when HR/Management selects that Temporary as the permanent replacement. The same employee record becomes Permanent (no duplicate); the new Permanent assignment inherits the departing Permanent's salary automatically (the salary in effect on the last paid day); HR enters and confirms nothing. The audit history records the change, effective date, previous and new type, salary, affected assignments and the actor. The Temporary's cover on that position ends with it; the cover and every earlier record are kept. |
| N3 | A **Waiting List** person is appointed through the existing status workflow (Waiting to Active, with its registration-workflow gate), assigned, and recorded in the same transaction. If any step fails, nothing is left behind. The salary is the one HR enters (the existing rule for a Permanent successor); it is never assumed to be inherited. |
| N4 | **Transfer of a Permanent between assignments** is the current two steps: Stop the old assignment, then Assign the new position, facility or company. The old assignment's dates, salary history and audit trail are kept; it is never overwritten. |
| N5 | **Temporary payment:** the expected amount is calculated automatically and **prefilled**. A different entered amount shows a clear warning with the calculated amount beside it, but is **permitted** (manual override, not blocked). The calculated amount, the amount actually paid, the optional reason, the notes, the payment date and the actor are recorded. A payment approves nothing. |
| N6 | **Salary change within a month, Temporary cover:** split the month by the salary's effective date - the old salary before it, the new salary from that date on - using the real calendar days, without double-counting a date. |
| N7 (amended by U9) | **Effective date of the new Permanent:** HR selects it, and it is **inclusive** - that day is part of the new Permanent assignment and salary. If HR selects 10 October, 10 October is paid at the new salary; it is never moved to 11 October, and a later date is never backdated. When HR leaves it empty it is the day after the departing employee's last paid day. It must be after that last paid day (that day is paid to the departing employee), so an earlier or equal date is refused with an explanation, not corrected. |
| N8 | **Waiting List fallback:** no earlier approved rule combined appointment and assignment, so the workflow does both in one safe transaction, respecting the existing eligibility checks, permissions and validation (a new registration that has not completed the registration workflow is refused exactly as anywhere else). |
| N9 | **The Temporary payment counts the full period** during which the assignment actually existed, not only individually verified workdays. Attendance records elsewhere keep their meaning. |
| N10 | **Early termination:** if the Temporary assignment ends before its planned end, only the days it actually existed count. Both the planned end date and the actual end date are kept. |
| N11 | **Monthly proration:** monthly salary / real days of the month (28, 29, 30, 31; leap February included) x the calendar days the assignment existed in that month, salary changes applied by effective date (N6). Example: 600 over 10 days of a 30-day month = 200. |
| **U8 (final)** | **One coverage period for entitlement and deduction.** The Temporary coverage period is its actual start date to its actual end date, both inclusive. The same period determines the Temporary's entitlement and the related deduction from the Permanent. The entitlement is calculated first (salary in effect on each day, real days of each month); the deduction is then the existing approved rule - the month's entitlement deducted once, only after approval, never a day that was already unpaid leave. No new penalty formula, rate or policy exists or was invented. Verified attendance days are not a substitute for the coverage period. The entitlement and the deduction are separate figures in the data and in the audit trail. Duplicate deductions and duplicate charges for one position and period are prevented, and a Temporary is never paid twice for one day. |
| **U9 (final)** | **Employment type now, payroll from the effective date.** When the selection is saved, `employment_type` changes to Permanent at once and the employee record shows it at once. The new Permanent assignment, its salary and its payroll calculation begin on the selected effective date, inclusive. Four things stay distinct: (1) the employee's current employment type; (2) the assignment's start date; (3) the salary's effective date; (4) the payroll period in which amounts are calculated. The immediate type change never causes pay before the effective date, and earlier periods' calculations are not rewritten by it. |
| **2026-10-27 (consolidated)** | **Gaps** are allowed: the successor starts on the effective date and the days between the last paid day and a later effective date are not paid to the successor. The Excel column `Verified workdays` keeps its name and meaning (verified attendance days) while the Temporary payment uses coverage-period days; the Excel format is preserved. **Replace Employee** keeps accepting any Active employee. **No new payment-approval gate** is added; the current payment workflow and warning behaviour stay. |
| **U1 (final, 2026-10-27)** | **One employee cannot work two assignments whose actual shift times overlap; different, non-overlapping shifts on the same calendar day are allowed.** When HR tries to give Temporary work (a cover) to an employee who already has an assignment of their own over the same days, the system refuses and shows the owner-approved **Somali warning** (HR must first cancel/end the earlier assignment and assign another employee to the earlier position). It never cancels an assignment, replaces an employee, changes the employment type or touches payroll by itself. Enforced in the backend (any caller) and in the form. **Shift times:** the system records only a Day / Night shift per assignment, never clock times; two assignments are treated as different only when BOTH shifts are recorded and different. A missing or equal shift is never assumed to differ: the refusal says what to correct (record the shifts). Assignment and salary history are preserved. Automatic Permanent-to-Temporary conversion is **not** implemented. |
| **U12 (final, 2026-10-27)** | **Overlapping Temporary covers are REJECTED, not shared out.** A new cover, or a later last day on an existing one, is refused when its dates overlap another Temporary cover of the **same position** (always), or of the **same Temporary employee on another position** (unless the two positions are on different recorded shifts). The refusal names the conflicting cover. Dates are compared inclusively on both ends; the cover being edited is never its own conflict. The earlier "the earlier cover owns the overlapping days" allocation was **removed**: every cover counts all of its own days. Enforced in the backend (create, end/extend, assignment edits) and in the form. |
| **Monthly payment list (final, 2026-10-27)** | **Temporary workers are listed** in the monthly payment list (Excel), each on a row directly **beneath the Permanent employee they covered** (the same adjacency the payroll run workbook uses), linked by the recorded cover - never by name. The existing columns A-P and their order are unchanged; five columns were added at the right. The Temporary amount is the cover's entitlement for the month (the same calculation as the deduction); the balance is what the cover earned up to the end of the month minus the payments recorded up to then. The existing totals stay the Permanent employees'; the Temporary total and the combined total are written separately, so nothing is counted twice, and the Temporary amounts reconcile to the deductions taken from the Permanents. |

## 4. Temporary and Replacement calculation rules, side by side

| | Temporary replacement (cover) | Replacement (assignment type) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Cover for a period; the Permanent assignment continues | Takes over the position; the Permanent assignment ends |
| Whose salary | The covered Permanent's, from that assignment's salary history | The replaced Permanent's, copied as the Replacement's own salary row |
| Days that count | **U8: every calendar day from the cover's actual start to its actual last day, both inclusive** (not past today, not past the covered assignment; overlapping covers do not exist - they are refused when saved, U12) | The calendar days of the Replacement's assignment dates, A3 |
| Temporary's entitlement | Salary in effect on each day / real days of the month, each month rounded once (`TemporaryExpectedPayment`) | A normal pay line |
| Deduction from the Permanent | **U8: the same period.** The month's entitlement, deducted once, after approval, minus days already unpaid leave (`ReplacementCostService`) | Not applicable: the Permanent is paid until the last covered day |
| Permanent salary changes mid-month | **N6: split by effective date** | **A9: new salary for the whole month** |
| Own pay line | None; the cost is shown beneath the Permanent in the payroll Excel; paid by recorded payments | Normal pay line |

Worked example (the same facts under each rule): Permanent salary 300 until 24 September and 400 from 25 September, 30-day month. A Temporary who covered 21-30 September: entitlement **(4 x 300 + 6 x 400) / 30 = 120.00**, and the deduction from the Permanent is the same 120.00 (pinned by tests). A Replacement holding the position all September: **A9 = 400 for the whole month**, not 300 for 24 days and 400 for 6.

**The existing deduction rule, stated plainly (U8 point 5-6).** The deduction rule that already existed (Decision 8, 2026-10-10) is: *aggregate the approved replacement cost and deduct it once from the correct Permanent's payroll*. It has no rate, penalty or percentage of its own, so the deduction equals the entitlement of the same period. Nothing was added. The only reductions are the earlier approved rules that a day is never deducted twice: a day the Permanent was already on approved unpaid leave, and a day outside the covered assignment's own dates.

## 5. Conversion rules and evidence

### 5.1 Temporary to Permanent - the approved scenario (N2, N7, U9)
- **Approved and implemented:** only the scenario in N1/N2 (a departing Permanent is replaced by an existing Temporary who covered that position). It is not generalised to other conversions.
- **How it works:** one transaction ends the departing assignment, ends the person's cover on it, sets `employment_type` to `permanent` (audited from/to, effective date), creates the Permanent successor assignment from the effective date with the departing salary, and records the choice in `assignment_successions`.
- **Type now, pay later (U9):** the flag changes when the selection is saved. Before the effective date the person has no assignment of their own, so no payroll line exists for them; the cost of their cover keeps being deducted from the departing Permanent up to that Permanent's last paid day, exactly as before. Regenerating payroll for any earlier month gives the same lines.
- **Older paths kept:** a Replacement can still be edited to Permanent (audited); a Temporary confirmed as a Replacement still blocks payroll until corrected (A13).

### 5.2 Permanent to Temporary
- **No approved rule found.** Searched: the SRS, all HR audit reports and rule records, all four project conversation records, Git history and tests. The new flow never converts a Permanent into a Temporary (a Permanent chosen as a "Temporary" successor is refused with `SUCCESSOR_NOT_TEMPORARY`).
- **Existing behaviour (unchanged):** the flag can be edited (audited); the person's open assignments are not touched; payroll then **skips** that person's own assignments with a warning, so their pay line disappears. **U1 (final, section 3) does not change this and adds no automatic conversion:** it only refuses to give such a person Temporary cover work that overlaps their own assignment (Somali warning), and the reverse (an assignment that overlaps their cover). Whether reclassifying a Permanent employee should itself be blocked or guided was not asked; nothing was built for it.
