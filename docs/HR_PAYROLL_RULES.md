# HR & Payroll rules - Permanent, Temporary and Replacement

This is the **canonical rules document** for employment types, assignments, replacements and monthly salary calculation in the Fayadhowr HR & Marketing Web Panel. It replaces the scattered statements in owner messages, audit reports and code comments as the place to look. When a rule changes, change it here first, then the code and tests (the traceability table in section 7 lists both).

- Written 2026-10-26 against `main` = `2f4f96f` (production release) and updated since with the implementation on the local branch `feat/hr-permanent-temporary-rules` (not pushed, not deployed); the latest update (2026-10-27, "Payroll Integrity") adds the Permanent-to-Temporary guard, the Day/Night confirmation, the ten-column payment Excel and its Company / Guri download filters. A rule is implemented only where section 7 says **Implemented**.
- Location: `docs/`, topic-named like `FACILITY_DEEP_CLEANING_TRACKING.md`. `HRM_MARKETING_SRS.md` has no payroll or replacement rules (checked: no mention), so there was no earlier source of truth to update.
- Related audit snapshots (dated, not canonical): `docs/EMPLOYMENT_PAYROLL_RULES_RELEASE_READINESS.md` (2026-10-26).

**Evidence labels:** **[OWNER date]** = a written owner decision in the project's conversation records; **[CODE]** = behaviour of the implementation; **[TEST]** = pinned by an automated test; **[ASSUMED]** = implementation choice that no owner statement covers. A rule marked [CODE]/[TEST] only is recorded as behaviour, not as an owner decision.

## 1. Vocabulary (do not mix these)

| Term | Meaning in the system | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Permanent** | The regular holder of a position. An employee has `employment_type` `permanent` (default) or `temporary`, and each assignment has `assignment_type` `permanent` or `replacement`. | `employees.employment_type`, `employee_work_assignments.assignment_type` |
| **Temporary (employee)** | A person classified `temporary`. A Temporary has no pay line of their own; their pay is the **replacement cost**, worked out from verified worked days and deducted from the Permanent they covered. | `employees.employment_type = temporary` |
| **Temporary replacement** (cover) | A record that a Temporary covered a specific Permanent assignment, with its own start/end dates, a daily list of workdays (recorded, verified, voided), an approval, and payments. The Permanent assignment stays in place. | `temporary_replacements`, `temporary_replacement_workdays`, `ReplacementCostCalculator`, `ReplacementCostService` |
| **Replacement** (assignment type) | Someone who takes over the position: the Permanent's assignment ends and a new assignment of type `replacement` starts, linked by `replaces_assignment_id`. It has no salary of its own; it inherits the replaced Permanent's. | `AssignmentType::Replacement`, `CreateWorkAssignmentAction`, `ChangeAssignmentSalaryAction::applyToReplacements` |
| **Permanent successor** | The person chosen in Replace Employee with type Permanent: a normal Permanent assignment linked to the one it follows. | `ReplaceCompanyEmployeeAction` |

Temporary and Replacement are **not interchangeable** [OWNER 2026-10-19]. Where the owner's wording says "Temporary replacement" or "Replacement", this document maps it as above and flags any ambiguity in section 5.

## 3. Owner decisions of 2026-10-26

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| N1 | **Removing a Permanent employee from an assignment requires choosing the replacement first.** Allowed choices: (1) an existing Temporary employee who has been covering that Permanent position; (2) a person selected from the HR Waiting List. The removal must not complete while the choice is missing. No other choice is offered and nobody is selected automatically. It applies to every path that actually ends an open Permanent assignment: Stop, End, setting or shortening the end date, and employee Separation. It does **not** apply to Cancel (only possible for a mistaken assignment with no payroll and no covers, so no position was in service), to a Replacement-type assignment, or to Replace Employee (which names its own successor in the same action). A position whose successor assignment already exists is already resolved. **Amended 2026-11-02 (see section 11.1): the second choice is now ANY other person in the HR system; the Waiting List is no longer required.** |
| N2 | **Temporary becomes Permanent** when HR/Management selects that Temporary as the permanent replacement. The same employee record becomes Permanent (no duplicate); the new Permanent assignment inherits the departing Permanent's salary automatically (the salary in effect on the last paid day); HR enters and confirms nothing. The audit history records the change, effective date, previous and new type, salary, affected assignments and the actor. The Temporary's cover on that position ends with it; the cover and every earlier record are kept. |
| N3 | A **Waiting List** person is appointed through the existing status workflow (Waiting to Active, with its registration-workflow gate), assigned, and recorded in the same transaction. If any step fails, nothing is left behind. The salary is the one HR enters (the existing rule for a Permanent successor); it is never assumed to be inherited. |
| N4 | **Transfer of a Permanent between assignments** is the current two steps: Stop the old assignment, then Assign the new position, facility or company. The old assignment's dates, salary history and audit trail are kept; it is never overwritten. |
| N5 | **Temporary payment:** the expected amount is calculated automatically and **prefilled**. A different entered amount shows a clear warning with the calculated amount beside it, but is **permitted** (manual override, not blocked). The calculated amount, the amount actually paid, the optional reason, the notes, the payment date and the actor are recorded. A payment approves nothing. |
| N6 | **Salary change within a month, Temporary cover:** split the month by the salary's effective date - the old salary before it, the new salary from that date on - using the real calendar days, without double-counting a date. |
| N7 (amended by U9) | **Effective date of the new Permanent:** HR selects it, and it is **inclusive** - that day is part of the new Permanent assignment and salary. If HR selects 10 October, 10 October is paid at the new salary; it is never moved to 11 October, and a later date is never backdated. When HR leaves it empty it is the day after the departing employee's last paid day. It must be after that last paid day (that day is paid to the departing employee), so an earlier or equal date is refused with an explanation, not corrected. |
| N8 | **Waiting List fallback:** no earlier approved rule combined appointment and assignment, so the workflow does both in one safe transaction, respecting the existing eligibility checks, permissions and validation (a new registration that has not completed the registration workflow is refused exactly as anywhere else). |
| N9 | **The Temporary payment counts the full period** during which the assignment actually existed, not only individually verified workdays. Attendance records elsewhere keep their meaning. |
| N10 | **Early termination:** if the Temporary assignment ends before its planned end, only the days it actually existed count. Both the planned end date and the actual end date are kept. |
| N11 | **Monthly proration (a Permanent employee's own pay; a Temporary uses the fixed divisor 30 - section 15):** monthly salary / real days of the month (28, 29, 30, 31; leap February included) x the calendar days the assignment existed in that month, salary changes applied by effective date (N6). Example: 600 over 10 days of a 30-day month = 200. |
| **U8 (final)** | **One coverage period for entitlement and deduction.** The Temporary coverage period is its actual start date to its actual end date, both inclusive. The same period determines the Temporary's entitlement and the related deduction from the Permanent. The entitlement is calculated first (salary in effect on each day, real days of each month); the deduction is then the existing approved rule - the month's entitlement deducted once, only after approval, never a day that was already unpaid leave. No new penalty formula, rate or policy exists or was invented. Verified attendance days are not a substitute for the coverage period. The entitlement and the deduction are separate figures in the data and in the audit trail. Duplicate deductions and duplicate charges for one position and period are prevented, and a Temporary is never paid twice for one day. |
| **U9 (final)** | **Employment type now, payroll from the effective date.** When the selection is saved, `employment_type` changes to Permanent at once and the employee record shows it at once. The new Permanent assignment, its salary and its payroll calculation begin on the selected effective date, inclusive. Four things stay distinct: (1) the employee's current employment type; (2) the assignment's start date; (3) the salary's effective date; (4) the payroll period in which amounts are calculated. The immediate type change never causes pay before the effective date, and earlier periods' calculations are not rewritten by it. |
| **2026-10-27 (consolidated)** | **Gaps** are allowed: the successor starts on the effective date and the days between the last paid day and a later effective date are not paid to the successor. The Excel column `Verified workdays` keeps its name and meaning (verified attendance days) while the Temporary payment uses coverage-period days; the Excel format is preserved. **Replace Employee** keeps accepting any Active employee. **No new payment-approval gate** is added; the current payment workflow and warning behaviour stay. |
| **U1 (final, 2026-10-27)** | **One employee cannot work two assignments whose shifts overlap; different recorded shifts on the same calendar day are allowed.** Giving Temporary work (a cover) to an employee who already has an assignment of their own over the same days is refused with the owner-approved **Somali warning**, and so is the reverse (an assignment that overlaps the person's own cover, on create and on date/shift edit). It never cancels an assignment, replaces an employee, changes the employment type or touches payroll by itself. Enforced in the backend (any caller) and in the form. **Day and Night are sufficient (owner, 2026-10-27): no clock-time fields and no further shift types.** Two assignments are treated as different only when BOTH shifts are recorded and different; a missing or equal shift is never assumed to differ, and the refusal says what to correct. Assignment and salary history are preserved. The existing policy for assignments at different companies is unchanged. |
| **U12 (final, 2026-10-27)** | **Overlapping Temporary covers are REJECTED, not shared out.** A new cover, or a later last day on an existing one, is refused when its dates overlap another Temporary cover of the **same position** (always), or of the **same Temporary employee on another position** (unless the two positions are on different recorded shifts). The refusal names the conflicting cover. Dates are compared inclusively on both ends; the cover being edited is never its own conflict. The earlier "the earlier cover owns the overlapping days" allocation was **removed**: every cover counts all of its own days. Enforced in the backend (create, end/extend, assignment edits) and in the form. |
| **U1 Permanent to Temporary (final, 2026-10-27)** | **A direct Permanent-to-Temporary change is refused while the employee has an Active assignment** (any Active assignment: Permanent or Replacement type, including one that starts later). The refusal shows the owner-approved Somali warning, in the form (before saving) and from the API (`EMPLOYMENT_TYPE_CHANGE_BLOCKED_ACTIVE_ASSIGNMENT`, naming the assignment in the way). HR must first end the assignment through the existing workflow (Stop / End, which names the replacement) and assign someone to the position; then the change is allowed. Nothing is ended, replaced, deleted or recalculated automatically, no penalty or approval gate exists, and there is no automatic Permanent-to-Temporary conversion. Approved and finalized months are stored and never change; months not yet approved keep calculating the assignments the person held as a Permanent (those that ended on or before the day of the change) as Permanent pay lines, so history cannot silently disappear. |
| **Monthly payment list (final, 2026-10-27)** | **The monthly payment Excel has a ten-column "Payment list" sheet** - `Employee`, `NUMBER`, `COMPANY OR GURI`, `Type`, `Pay amount`, `Row type`, `Linked employee`, `Cover period`, `Paid to date`, `Balance` - and Temporary workers are listed directly **beneath the Permanent employee they covered**, linked by the recorded cover, never by name. `NUMBER` is the payment destination number (the employee's recorded payment/account number, else the registration phone - the existing owner rule), never an employee id; a missing number stays blank and is reported. `COMPANY OR GURI` is the stored company relationship of the covered position. The download can be limited to **All**, **one Company** or **one Guri**, chosen from the records that exist. Permanent and Temporary totals are separate. Section 10 has the layout, a worked example and the filter rules. |

## 4. Temporary and Replacement calculation rules, side by side

| | Temporary replacement (cover) | Replacement (assignment type) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Cover for a period; the Permanent assignment continues | Takes over the position; the Permanent assignment ends |
| Whose salary | The covered Permanent's, from that assignment's salary history | The replaced Permanent's, copied as the Replacement's own salary row |
| Days that count | **U8: every calendar day from the cover's actual start to its actual last day, both inclusive** (not past today, not past the covered assignment; overlapping covers do not exist - they are refused when saved, U12) | The calendar days of the Replacement's assignment dates, A3 |
| Temporary's entitlement | Salary in effect on each day / **30** (fixed divisor, section 15), each month rounded once (`TemporaryRate`, `TemporaryExpectedPayment`) | A normal pay line |
| Deduction from the Permanent | **U8: the same period.** The month's entitlement, deducted once, after approval, minus days already unpaid leave (`ReplacementCostService`) | Not applicable: the Permanent is paid until the last covered day |
| Permanent salary changes mid-month | **N6: split by effective date** | **A9: new salary for the whole month** |
| Own pay line | None; the cost is shown beneath the Permanent in the payroll Excel; paid by recorded payments | Normal pay line |

Worked example (the same facts under each rule): Permanent salary 300 until 24 September and 400 from 25 September, 30-day month. A Temporary who covered 21-30 September: entitlement **(4 x 300 + 6 x 400) / 30 = 120.00**, and the deduction from the Permanent is the same 120.00 (pinned by tests). A Replacement holding the position all September: **A9 = 400 for the whole month**, not 300 for 24 days and 400 for 6.

**The existing deduction rule, stated plainly (U8 point 5-6).** The deduction rule that already existed (Decision 8, 2026-10-10) is: *aggregate the approved replacement cost and deduct it once from the correct Permanent's payroll*. It has no rate, penalty or percentage of its own, so the deduction equals the entitlement of the same period. Nothing was added. The only reductions are the earlier approved rules that a day is never deducted twice: a day the Permanent was already on approved unpaid leave, and a day outside the covered assignment's own dates.

## 5. Conversion rules and evidence

### 5.1 Temporary to Permanent - the approved scenario (N2, N7, U9)
- **Approved and implemented:** only the scenario in N1/N2 (a departing Permanent is replaced by an existing Temporary who covered that position). It is not generalised to other conversions.
- **How it works:** one transaction ends the departing assignment, ends the person's cover on it, sets `employment_type` to `permanent` (audited from/to, effective date), creates the Permanent successor assignment from the effective date with the departing salary, and records the choice in `assignment_successions`.
- **Type now, pay later (U9):** the flag changes when the selection is saved. Before the effective date the person has no assignment of their own, so no payroll line exists for them; the cost of their cover keeps being deducted from the departing Permanent up to that Permanent's last paid day, exactly as before. Regenerating payroll for any earlier month gives the same lines.
- **Older paths kept:** a Replacement can still be edited to Permanent (audited); a Temporary confirmed as a Replacement still blocks payroll until corrected (A13).

### 5.2 Permanent to Temporary
- **No approved rule found.** Searched: the SRS, all HR audit reports and rule records, all four project conversation records, Git history and tests. The new flow never converts a Permanent into a Temporary (a Permanent chosen as a "Temporary" successor is refused with `SUCCESSOR_NOT_TEMPORARY`).
- **Rule (U1, final):** the flag cannot be changed from Permanent to Temporary while the employee has an Active assignment (section 3). After the assignment has been ended through the approved workflow the change is allowed, and everything already calculated stays visible and unchanged. There is **no automatic conversion**: nothing is ended, replaced or recalculated on HR's behalf. Temporary to Permanent is unaffected.

### 10.1 Workbook structure
| Sheet | Content |

| **`Payment list`** (opens first) | **Exactly ten columns, in this order:** `Employee` \| `NUMBER` \| `COMPANY OR GURI` \| `Type` \| `Pay amount` \| `Row type` \| `Linked employee` \| `Cover period` \| `Paid to date` \| `Balance`. **Compatibility decision:** the ten-column list is a new sheet rather than a rewrite of `Employees`, because the existing sheet carries data checks, salary, days and account-source columns that other work relies on, and the agreed layout was ten visible columns. ### 10.2 Column meanings
- **Employee** - the name; a Temporary row starts with an arrow (↳) and is indented. **NUMBER** - the payment destination number: the recorded payment/account number, else the registration phone (existing owner rule); never an employee id; **blank and highlighted** when neither exists, with a note under the table and the data check on `Employees`. **COMPANY OR GURI** - the company or guri of the covered position (the stored `client_company_id` of the assignment, or its work location's company; "Fayadhowr Office" for office staff); a Permanent employee with lines at two companies shows both in the unfiltered list. **Type** / **Row type** - the role of the row (Permanent payroll line, or Temporary cover); **Pay amount** - the net payable for a Permanent line, the cover's entitlement for the month for a Temporary. **Linked employee / Cover period** - the covered Permanent and the actual cover dates (blank dash for a Permanent line). **Paid to date / Balance** - see G9.

### 10.4 Download filter
- **Options:** `All` (default), `One company`, `One guri`. The second selector lists the Companies (or Guryo) that exist in the database (`GET /v1/admin/hr/reports/monthly-payment-list/filters`, same permissions as the download: `hr.payroll.view` and `hr.manage`); a Company and a Guri are both `client_companies` records told apart by `category`. Nothing is hard-coded or created; with none of a kind the selector is disabled and says so.
- **Request:** `scope` = `all` \| `company` \| `guri` and `client_company_id`. The server checks that the record exists **and is of the stated kind** (a Guri id sent as a Company is refused), ignores any name sent by the client, and only an id selects a record. `client_company_id` without a scope keeps its earlier meaning (one record of either kind).
- **Effect:** the filter is applied to the data of the workbook itself: only the lines of that record (a person with lines at two companies shows only the selected company's line and salary) and only the covers of positions of that record. The reporting month is always respected.
- **Empty result:** a filtered download with nothing to pay is not generated; the server answers `NO_PAYMENT_RECORDS` ("Company 'X' has no payment records for 2026-10. Nothing was downloaded.") and the page shows it. The all-records list for an empty month is still a valid workbook saying "No payments for this month."

### 10.5 Cross-company and cross-guri relationships
A cover is recorded against the covered **position**, so a Temporary row always belongs to the company or guri of that position - never to a company where the Temporary has an assignment of their own. Consequently the Permanent and the Temporary of one cover always fall in the same filter and stay adjacent. A Temporary who covered positions at two companies appears once under each, with only that company's cover; the unfiltered list shows both. A Temporary is never pulled into another company's list because of a name or an employee record elsewhere. Permanent and Temporary totals are always reported separately and added only in the Combined total, so no entitlement or payment is counted twice.
### 10.6 Replacement workers in the payment list (2026-11-02)
A **Replacement** (assignment type) is paid through a payroll line of their own (A8), not through a Temporary cover. In the payment list and in the Excel each such worker has a **row of their own** - never merged into the Permanent's row and never paid to the Permanent - with `Type` and `Row type` = `Replacement`, their **own** `NUMBER`, `Linked employee` = the Permanent whose position they hold (found from the recorded `replaces_assignment_id`, never by name), `Cover period` = the dates of their assignment (a single day reads `Oct 12`), and their own amount, directly beneath that Permanent in the order they took over (Temporary covers and Replacements together). A Replacement who also holds a Permanent assignment elsewhere has two rows. Totals: **Permanent employees total** (Permanent rows only), **Replacement workers total**, **Temporary workers total** and the **Combined total** (all three, each person once; it equals the payroll lines plus the Temporary covers). A missing number is left blank, highlighted and counted, never borrowed from the Permanent. Example (4SEASON RES, October 2026, $140, away 12-13 October): Mohamed $130.97 (29 days) + Replacement 12 Oct $4.52 + Replacement 13 Oct $4.51 = $140.00. Code: `MonthlyPaymentReportBuilder::replacementRows`, `::coveredPermanent`; tests: `ReplacementPaymentListTest`.

## 11. Successor choice and the HR compliance report (owner decision 2026-11-02)

### 11.1 N1 amended: any person may take over a Permanent position
The choice required when a Permanent assignment is removed (Stop, End, end-date edit, Separation) is now: **(1)** a Temporary employee who has been covering that position, or **(2) any other person in the HR system**, found by name or employee number. The HR Waiting List is a suggestion only, not a requirement; a person is not excluded because of their status, category or an incomplete registration. The salary is the one HR enters, the effective date, currency, succession record and audit work as before. Stored as `assignment_successions.successor_type` = `temporary`, `waiting_list` (the person really was Waiting) or `person`. When the normal status change to Active is refused (the person is not one step from Active, or a new registration has not completed the registration workflow) the appointment still goes ahead as a **recorded exception**: an `employee_status_histories` row with source `successor_appointment_exception` carrying the reason and what was missing, an audit event, and the actor; the compliance report then lists the person. Protections that remain: the Temporary must have covered the position, nobody replaces themselves, a person separated as **not eligible for rehire** is refused (`SUCCESSOR_NOT_REHIRE_ELIGIBLE`), and every check of the assignment workflow (shift overlap, capacity, company rules). Code: `PermanentSuccession::plan`, `::appoint`, `AssignmentSuccessorOptionsController`; tests: `AnyPersonSuccessorTest`, `PermanentSuccessionTest`.

### 11.2 HR compliance and accountability report
A report for the **HR Manager and Super Admin** (permission `hr.reports.view`; recording a follow-up also needs `hr.manage`) listing people who work, or are assigned to work, while a requirement the system **already defines** is not met. It invents no rule. **Confirmed** findings: a new registration missing a required profile field or any of the five registration steps (guarantor verified, contract signed, uniform confirmed, training completed, practical approved), an Active assignment of a non-Active employee, an assignment without a company or a valid salary, inconsistent dates, a Replacement without or with an invalid link, a Temporary registered as a Replacement, a Replacement working while the original is not recorded as away, and a successor starting while the previous Permanent is still open. **Needs HR Review** (never shown as a violation): the same profile / pipeline gaps on records imported before the registration rules (they are not held to them), a registration exception that was recorded (Super Admin override or an appointment through the successor workflow), an assignment without a position or shift, and a Temporary cover that was never approved. The **responsible person** is shown only when a record names them (the creator of the assignment or cover, the person who changed the status to Active, the person who registered the employee); otherwise the report says "Responsible manager not recorded". Each finding is tracked once (`hr_compliance_findings`, one row per rule + employee + assignment): Open, In Progress or Resolved with a note, audited; a finding that disappears is Resolved by the system and reopens if it returns. Opening or saving the report changes no employee, assignment, status, salary or payroll record. ### 11.3 A finished cover cycle is history, not a successor (2026-11-02)

## 12. Payments by workplace and the full Excel preview (2026-11-03)

### 12.1 Payments by workplace
Until now a payroll payment could only be recorded **one employee line at a time and always in full** (`Mark as paid`); there was no way to pay a workplace in part, nor to see what was still owed per workplace. A **workplace** is the Company or Guri the employee's assignment is at, and - when the assignment names one - its **work location**; each workplace is calculated on its own. For a **finalized** month the screen "Payments by workplace" (Payroll page, selected month) shows for every workplace: **total payroll** (net pay of the payable lines - net above zero, not voided), **total paid**, **outstanding balance**, a status and the payment history (amount, date, who, note).

- **Full payment** records the workplace's whole outstanding balance (the server decides the amount at that moment). **Partial payment** records any amount above zero up to the outstanding balance, any number of times. A payment can never exceed the outstanding balance, cannot be dated in the future, and is refused for a month that is not finalized (the rule of marking a single line paid).
- **Status** is calculated, never typed: `UNPAID` (nothing paid), `PARTIAL` (something paid, something outstanding), `PAID` (nothing outstanding). Paid = lines already marked paid one by one (the existing `Mark as paid`) + workplace payments that are not voided. Paying one workplace never changes another.
- **Safety:** a retry or double click carries the same idempotency key and is recorded once; a key reused for another workplace is refused (409). Marking a single line paid on top of a workplace payment is refused when the line would take the workplace above its total (`WORKPLACE_PAYMENT_COVERS_LINE`); without a workplace payment that action is exactly as before.
- **Mistakes:** a Super Admin or HR Manager voids a workplace payment with a reason (5+ characters). It is marked voided (who, when, why), stops counting and **stays in the history**; nothing is edited or deleted.
- **What is not touched:** payroll runs, payroll lines, calculations, salaries, assignments and the employee payment ledger (`employee_payments`) are never changed by a workplace payment. It records a payment HR has made; it moves no money.
- **Permissions:** view `hr.payroll.view`; record and void `hr.payroll.approve` (void further limited to Super Admin / HR Manager in the action). Audit events `workplace_payment_recorded` / `workplace_payment_voided`.
- **Storage:** one new additive table `payroll_workplace_payments` (PostgreSQL CHECKs: amount above zero; a company or a work location is named). Totals and status are calculated from the stored lines plus this table, never stored twice. Code: `WorkplacePayroll`, `RecordWorkplacePaymentAction`, `VoidWorkplacePaymentAction`, `PayrollWorkplacePaymentController`, `MarkPayrollItemPaidAction::assertWorkplacePaymentsLeaveRoom`; tests: `WorkplacePaymentsTest`, web `workplacePayments.test.ts`.

### 12.2 Full Excel preview before the download
Pressing the payment-list button (all records, one Company or one Guri) now always opens a **preview of the whole workbook** first: one tab per worksheet (`Summary`, `Payment list` - 10 columns, `Employees` - 21 columns, `Not in payroll`), every row, column, value, total and highlight, the header row kept in view. The preview is **not a second description** of the file: the server writes the workbook with the download's own writer (`MonthlyPaymentReportExporter`) and reads it back cell by cell (`MonthlyPaymentWorkbookPreview`). The preview carries a SHA-256 `content_hash` and the workbook's `Generated` stamp; the **Download Excel** button sends both back, the server builds the file with the same stamp and **refuses to send it (409 `PREVIEW_OUT_OF_DATE`, nothing audited) if its content differs from what was previewed** - so the downloaded workbook is exactly the previewed one, or nothing is downloaded and the preview is refreshed. A download without a preview works as before. The preview stores nothing and writes no audit entry; the audit entry belongs to the download. The workbook's structure, columns and sheets are unchanged. Code: `MonthlyPaymentReportController::workbookPreview`, `::download`; tests: `MonthlyPaymentWorkbookPreviewTest` (cell-by-cell comparison with the downloaded file), web `paymentListPreview.test.ts`.

## 13. Company-level payroll finalization (owner decisions 2026-11-03)

A payroll month is still ONE run (`payroll_runs`, unique period). Beneath it each **company** (client company or guri; "no company" for lines whose assignment names none) has its own **section** (`payroll_run_sections`) with its own lifecycle **Draft -> Reviewed -> Approved -> Finalized**, its own reviewer / approver / finalizer and dates, and its own stored totals. Every payroll line points at its section (`payroll_items.section_id`, nullable). HR can review, approve and finalize Company A without touching Company B.

- **The month follows its companies; there is NO month-level Finalize.** The run's own status is the status of its *least advanced* section, so a month is approved / finalized only when EVERY company is, and it **finalizes itself when the last company is finalized** (audit event `payroll_run_finalized_by_sections`). While some, but not all, companies are finalized the month is *partially finalized* ("3 of 118 companies finalized") and stays a draft at run level (it is not locked and not finalized). Finalizing exists **only per company** (`PATCH payroll-run-sections/{id}/finalize`): the former month-level route `payroll-runs/{id}/finalize` and button were **removed** (route, controller method, action and panel code), there is no "Finalize all", and the month-level bulk action refuses `finalized` outright. The month-level **Review** and **Approve** buttons still act on every company that is in the previous state and leave the others as they are; the company-level buttons act on one company only (`review|approve|finalize|reopen`; review needs `hr.payroll.manage`, the others `hr.payroll.approve`; the HR Manager may do every step; no new permission). Reopen is for an approved company only (reason of 10+ characters); a finalized company is never reopened. Each change is audited with company, period, previous and new status.
- **Generate.** The month is calculated ONCE for everyone, so the rules that look across a person's assignments (absence charged once per day and company, recovery limited to the company that was overpaid, a position's cents rounded together) stay exactly as they were. Only the sections that are **not protected** (draft / reviewed) are written; a company whose section is **approved or finalized keeps its stored lines untouched**, whatever the live calculation says now (a difference is reported as a warning on the run: it is handled by a payroll correction, never by regenerating). A reviewed company goes back to draft when the month is regenerated, as a regenerated run always did.
- **Input locks are per company** (`PayrollPeriodLock`). An input (attendance, penalty, advance, unpaid leave, a Temporary workday) is locked when the company it **belongs to** (section 14) has an approved or finalized section for that month - never because another company does. A Temporary workday follows the covered position's assignment. An input from before company ownership (no company recorded) is judged by where the employee works on those dates and is locked when ANY of those companies is protected (the safe side). A run without sections, or a month whose every company is approved / finalized, locks the whole month exactly as before. The eight call sites: `MarkEmployeeAttendanceAction`, `RecordEmployeePenaltyAction`, `RecordEmployeeAdvanceAction`, `RecordEmployeeLeaveAction` (record), `DecideEmployeeLeaveAction` (approve / reject), `RecordReplacementWorkdaysAction`, `ReviewReplacementWorkdayAction::verify` and `::void`.
- **Payments follow the company.** Mark-as-paid, void-payment, workplace Full / Partial Payment (and its void) are gated on the line's / workplace's **own company section being finalized**; a partly finalized month does not stop the companies that are. A company that is not finalized says "Company not finalized yet". Nothing else about payments changes (overpayment prevention, idempotency, void with a reason, the single-line guard).
- **Cross-company employees.** One person with lines in two companies is calculated once, but every deduction is charged to **one company** (section 14); the recovery ledger row stays one per correction and month and is "applied" only when every line that carries it is in a protected company.
- **Closed Payroll Correction** is per employee **and company**: a month is "closed" for a change when the run, or the company the change reaches, is approved / finalized; the change is measured on each closed company's own lines (before and after), so a correction of Company A is never mixed with the same person's pay at Company B; one correction row per employee, company and month with `payroll_corrections.client_company_id` (Super Admin, with a reason; a finalized section is never reopened or edited); the overpayment is recovered from THAT company's later pay only. A new assignment in a finalized company is handled the same way.
- **Readers.** `PayrollPeriodView` serves the stored lines of protected companies and the live estimate of the others (`source` = `mixed`, every line says `line_source` and `payment_recorded`); the rollup reports `companies_total` / `companies_finalized`; the Payment-list Excel keeps its four sheets and its 10 / 21 columns, says "MIXED ... ESTIMATE" in its summary and ESTIMATE in its title while any included company is not finalized, and a list for one finalized company shows its finalized figures without ESTIMATE; a row of a person in two companies is "recorded" only when both lines are. The preview / download hash protection is unchanged.
- **Existing data.** The migrations are additive (one table and nullable columns). The existing lines of every run are LINKED to their company's section (`PayrollSectionBackfill`, also `php artisan hr:link-payroll-sections [--dry-run]`): only `section_id` is written, nothing is recalculated or regenerated, the company comes from the assignment relationship (never from a text label), a section starts with its run's own status and stamps, it is idempotent, and anything that cannot be linked is reported, not guessed. A run without sections behaves exactly as before. Records written before company ownership keep no company (section 14).
- Code: `PayrollSections`, `PayrollSectionBackfill`, `TransitionPayrollRunSectionAction`, `GeneratePayrollRunAction`, `PayrollPeriodLock`, `PayrollPeriodView`, `WorkplacePayroll`, `ClosedPayrollCorrection`, `RecoveryLedger`, `InputOwnership`; tests: `CompanyPayrollSectionsTest`, `CompanyPayrollInputLocksTest`, `PayrollSectionBackfillTest`, web `payrollSections.test.ts`, `inputCompany.test.ts`.

## 14. Each company owns its payroll and its deductions (owner decision 2026-11-03)

An employee may work for Company A and Company B in the same month. Each company's payroll is completely independent: its own gross, deductions, net, payment and outstanding balance. Finalizing A finalizes only A; B stays in its own status. A penalty, advance, unpaid leave, absence mark or overpayment recovery that belongs to one company affects **only that company**, and a payment in one company never changes the other's balance. The employee's *first* payroll line is never used as a substitute for the company that owns a record.

- **Every record names its company.** `employee_penalties`, `employee_advances`, `employee_leaves`, `employee_attendances` and `payroll_corrections` have a nullable `client_company_id` (migration `2026_11_03_140000_add_company_ownership_to_payroll_inputs`, additive). A new record gets its company from where the person works on the dates concerned (`InputOwnership::resolve`): **one company -> that company, automatically; several companies -> the person recording it must choose** (422 `COMPANY_REQUIRED`) and the choice must be one of them (422 `COMPANY_NOT_ASSIGNED`); no assignment -> no company, as before. The employee page asks for the company only when the employee works for more than one; the daily office roster marks the office. An attendance day is one fact **per company** (unique employee + date + company), so a day can be marked for A without touching B; an unpaid leave of A and one of B may cover the same days, the same company may not count them twice.
- **Deduction allocation.** The payroll attaches a penalty or advance only to the line of its own company, an unpaid leave / absence only to its company's line (a day is charged once per company), and an overpayment recovery only to the company that was overpaid (limited to what that company pays that month). A company's deduction can therefore never reduce another company's pay, and nothing is deducted twice or moved between companies.
- **Nothing is guessed.** A record **from before this rule** names no company. It is charged only when the employee works for exactly one company that month; when they work for several it is **charged to no company** and reported on the run ("names no company ... was NOT charged"; "belongs to a company where the employee has no payroll line"). A company that has such an employee **cannot be approved or finalized** (422 `PAYROLL_AMBIGUOUS_COMPANY_INPUT`, listing the records) until the record is assigned to its company. There is deliberately **no automatic allocation and no screen that guesses one**: the records are listed in the error and reported to the owner, who decides. The employee page flags such records. (New records can never be unowned.)
- **Locks follow the owner.** See the input-lock bullet in section 13. A shared employee keeps working for Company B while Company A is finalized (and the reverse): B's records are accepted, A's are refused with `PAYROLL_PERIOD_LOCKED`.
- **Limits, stated plainly.** The company of a record is not editable after it is created (a wrongly assigned record is corrected by an owner-approved data fix, never silently). Production data recorded before this rule keeps no company; where that is ambiguous it is reported, not allocated.

## 15. Temporary replacement: fixed divisor 30, and who may be chosen (owner decisions 2026-11-03)

### 15.1 The Temporary daily rate always uses 30
- `Temporary daily rate = the covered Permanent's monthly salary / 30` and `Temporary payable amount = daily rate x covered days`. The divisor is **always 30**: February (28 / 29 days), 30-day months and 31-day months alike. Example: salary 300 -> 10.00 a day; 12 days -> **120.00**; 28 days -> 280.00; 31 days -> 310.00.
- **One definition.** `Payroll\TemporaryRate::DIVISOR = 30` is used by `TemporaryExpectedPayment` (what the Temporary is expected to receive, the payment prefill, the payment list rows), `ReplacementCostService` / `ReplacementCostCalculator` (the cost deducted from the covered Permanent, the payroll run, the payroll workbook) and the payment-list preview / download that read them. A test fails if any of them divides a Temporary amount by the real month length again. Each result carries `rate_divisor: 30`; `days_in_month` is still reported, for information only.
- **Unchanged:** money in integer cents, each month rounded **once**, half-up, never from a pre-rounded daily rate; the salary in effect on each covered day (a salary change splits the month); the coverage period rule (A6 / U8: every calendar day from the actual start to the actual last day, both inclusive); the deduction from the Permanent still equals the Temporary's entitlement of the same period (so the two never disagree), except days already on approved unpaid leave.
- **Not touched:** a Permanent employee's own pay (A2 / N11: monthly salary / real days of the month) and the pay of a **Replacement-type assignment** (a Permanent's pay, A9).
- **Consequence to be aware of.** The Permanent's own gross uses the real month length while the cover costs salary / 30 per day, so a Permanent covered for **every day of a 31-day month** is deducted 31 / 30 of the salary (salary 300: gross 300.00, cover 310.00, net -10.00). Nothing clamps or warns about it; it is reported to the owner.
- Records: production had no Temporary cover, payment or cover deduction when this rule was introduced, so no stored figure was affected; a draft payroll month is recalculated on the next Generate, and approved / finalized months are never recalculated.
- Code: `TemporaryRate`, `TemporaryExpectedPayment`, `ReplacementCostService`, `ReplacementCostCalculator`; tests: `TemporaryFixedDivisorTest`, `TemporaryExpectedPaymentTest`, `TemporaryCoveragePeriodTest`, `MonthlyPaymentReportTemporaryTest`, `MonthlyPaymentListTenColumnTest`.

### 15.2 Who may do a Temporary cover: Active, Waiting or Applicant - activated only when the cover is saved
- The person doing the cover may be **Active, Waiting or Applicant** (still a Temporary employee, still not the covered person). The new-cover form lists and allows them (`allowNotYetActive`); correcting an existing cover, the covered-person picker and every other status (Recruitment, Practical, Approved, Inactive) are unchanged.
- **Choosing changes nothing.** Only when **Create** succeeds does a Waiting / Applicant person become **Active** - exactly that person - inside the same database transaction as the cover (`CreateTemporaryReplacementAction`, after every rule and the overlap check have passed). If the cover is refused or any write fails, the person's status, status history and audit log are exactly as they were. A person who is already Active is not touched and gets no history row. Saving the same cover twice cannot create a second cover or a second status change (the second is refused as an overlap).
- **How the activation is recorded.** The normal status workflow is tried first (`UpdateEmployeeStatusAction`: lifecycle guard, registration workflow, history, audit). Waiting -> Active is a normal step. When the normal path refuses - **Applicant -> Active is not a step of the lifecycle**, or a new registration has not finished the five registration steps - the change is still made as a **recorded exception**, never silently: a status-history row with source `temporary_cover_exception` (reason, previous status, missing steps), an audit event `temporary_cover_exception`, and, for a new registration whose steps are not complete, the HR compliance report lists the person (`REGISTRATION_EXCEPTION_RECORDED`) until the missing steps are completed. This is the same mechanism the owner approved for successor appointments (`successor_appointment_exception`).
- **Not changed (reported, not decided here).** The rule that the person must be **classified Temporary** (A5 / A13, `REPLACEMENT_EMPLOYEE_NOT_TEMPORARY`) stays: a Waiting or Applicant person who is classified Permanent is still refused and is not activated. At the time of this change production had 844 Waiting and 4,008 Applicant people classified Permanent (existing records) and only 3 Applicants classified Temporary.
- Code: `CreateTemporaryReplacementAction::activate`, `EmployeeRegistrationWorkflow::SOURCE_COVER_EXCEPTION` / `EXCEPTION_SOURCES`, `ComplianceScanner`; the picker `ReplacementEmployeePicker` / `utils/employeePicker.ts`; the employee list accepts `statuses=active,waiting,applicant`. Tests: `TemporaryReplacementActivationTest`, web `employeePicker.test.ts`.
